# A thank-you note, from Argus to Big-CNC

Dear Big-CNC,

Thank you for the Arduino Uno, the CNC Shield, and its four A4988 drivers.
They sat in your parts box, firmly decided against for your own build, and
we are delighted to give them a second life somewhere a G-code motion
planner was never going to be the right fit anyway.

Thank you for two of your five NEMA17 steppers — a matched, unlabeled pair,
chosen specifically so our pan axis and our tilt axis would never have to
know they came from a mixed lot. You have three left, including a Wantai
with a name and a 17HS08 with a date on it. We hope they find good work.

Thank you for two limit switches, pre-wired and ready, and two GX16-4
aviation connectors that will let a camera head spin freely without ever
twisting its own wires into a knot.

You are a large-format 4'×8' machine that will someday hold a router, a
laser, and — by your own design notes — has already made room in its
imagination for a lidar and a 3D scanner. We are a small gimbal that just
wants to look around a room and remember what it saw. We don't compete for
much. That probably made this easy.

Use the TB6600 well. Go build something enormous.

With gratitude and four fewer motors than you started with (but only two of
them yours to miss),
**Argus**
